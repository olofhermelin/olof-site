Why Not a Zestimate?

Algorithms can't see your apartment.

Automated estimates struggle in New York, where two identical lines in the same building can trade 20% apart on light, floor, condition, and board policy. They don't know your renovation, your low maintenance, or that the comp next door had a compromised kitchen.

A hand-built valuation weighs the things the models can't: true comparable condition, building financials, abatement schedules, and how your specific line has traded over a decade.
