For Investors

Buy it, rent it, sell it. One agent for all three.

I help investors find the right New York property, put a tenant in it, and sell it when the time comes.

Get Started Call (917) 553-7447

An investment property in New York is a different purchase from a home. The building’s rental rules, the monthly costs and the realistic rent matter more than the view.

I work with first-time investors buying a single condo, families buying for a child at school here, overseas buyers, and owners of small multifamily buildings in Brooklyn and the Bronx.

What I Do For You

Find the right building

Condos with flexible rental rules, and two- to four-family houses. Most co-ops limit renting, so I check each building’s rules before you see it.

Run the real numbers

A realistic rent based on recent rentals nearby, set against common charges, taxes and closing costs.

Check tax abatements

Many newer condos have property tax breaks that end on a set date. I show you what the taxes become afterward.

Buy from anywhere

Video tours, digital signing, and introductions to attorneys and lenders who work with out-of-town and overseas buyers.

Rent it out

Once you close, I market the apartment and place your tenant.

Sell it well

When it is time to sell, including with a tenant in place, I price and market it. If you plan a 1031 exchange, I coordinate timing with your attorney and tax adviser.
