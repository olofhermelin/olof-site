The Residence

Contemporary living at the crossroads of three neighborhoods.

A contemporary one-bedroom condominium at the crossroads of Prospect Lefferts Gardens, Flatbush, and Prospect Park South. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the open living space with light, anchored by a kitchen with breakfast island, custom walnut cabinetry, and full-size appliances.

The king-size bedroom offers a double-width closet, and the spa-style bath features a deep soaking tub. A deeded storage unit and in-unit washer/dryer complete the home.

With a 421a tax abatement in place through 2034, monthly carrying costs stay remarkably low — a rare combination of new-development finishes and long-term value, two blocks from the Q/B at Church Avenue and a short walk to Prospect Park.
