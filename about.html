The Story

Great outcomes aren't found. They're worked for.

Olof genuinely loves interacting with people — getting to know his clients, understanding what they need, and advocating for it relentlessly through every step of a deal. That energy, paired with disciplined preparation, is why clients come back deal after deal.

A Licensed Real Estate Salesperson at Douglas Elliman, Olof has closed hundreds of transactions across sales and rentals — representing sellers, buyers, landlords, and tenants throughout Manhattan, Brooklyn, and Queens. He has worked both sides of nearly every kind of deal: co-op boards and condo offering plans, townhouse renovations, new development, off-market and estate sales.

His practice is built on discretion. Many of his listings are quietly marketed and never reach the portals; many of his buyers see homes before the public does. Every client — first rental or trophy townhouse — is represented with the same care, and with a resolute focus on their interests and their privacy.
