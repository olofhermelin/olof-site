For Landlords

Rent your apartment faster, to the right tenant.

Pricing, marketing, showings, screening and paperwork. One person handles it from the first photo to the signed lease.

Get Started Call (917) 553-7447

Every week an apartment sits empty costs you money. Renting it well comes down to three things: the right price on day one, marketing that gets the right people through the door, and careful screening.

I rent apartments for individual owners and small landlords across Manhattan, Brooklyn and the Bronx: condos, co-op sublets, townhouse floors and small buildings.

What I Do For You

Price it right

A rent recommendation based on what similar apartments nearby have actually rented for, not on asking prices.

Market it properly

Professional photos, a floor plan and a video tour, listed on the major rental sites and shared across the Douglas Elliman network.

Show it

I run every showing and open house, so you don’t have to.

Screen every applicant

Income, employment, credit and landlord references, checked the same way for everyone, in line with fair housing law.

Handle the board

In a condo or co-op, I prepare the tenant’s application package for the building and follow it through to approval.

Get the lease signed

I coordinate the lease, the deposits and the move-in date with you and your tenant.

Good To Know

Who pays the broker fee?

Since June 2025, New York City’s FARE Act says the broker is paid by whoever hires the broker. If you hire me to rent your apartment, the fee is yours and cannot be passed to the tenant. I will explain what that means for your rent and your net income before you decide anything.
